## Rust crates

The application uses permissively licensed crates including `clap`, `serde`,
`serde_json`, `serde_json_path`, `reqwest`, `tokio`, `tokio-util`, `windows`, `thiserror`, `uuid`,
`hound`, `chrono`, `parking_lot`, `async-trait`, `tempfile`, and their transitive
dependencies. Exact versions are locked in `Cargo.lock`. Release source
archives retain crate license metadata; generated binary packages continue to
include this notice and the project license.
